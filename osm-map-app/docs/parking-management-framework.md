# Parking Management Framework for a Commercial Building / Mall

## 1. Parking Access Zones (PAZ)

Divide the parking facility according to the primary vehicle access direction:

- PAZ-N — North Parking Access Zone
- PAZ-E — East Parking Access Zone
- PAZ-S — South Parking Access Zone
- PAZ-W — West Parking Access Zone

## 2. Vehicle Access

Each zone should identify:

- Vehicle entrance
- Vehicle exit
- Entry lane
- Exit lane
- Drive aisle
- Turning area
- Queuing area
- Emergency access
- Pedestrian crossing

## 3. Parking Accessibility

Rate each zone according to how easily vehicles can enter, circulate, park, and exit.

### High Accessibility

- Direct connection to entrance/exit
- Short travel distance
- Minimal congestion
- Easy turning and maneuvering

### Medium Accessibility

- Moderate travel distance
- Some circulation required
- Occasional congestion

### Low Accessibility

- Long travel distance
- Difficult maneuvering
- Congestion or bottlenecks
- Poor connection to entrances/exits

## 4. Parking Space Types

- Regular parking
- PWD/accessibility parking
- Senior citizen parking
- Family parking
- VIP/reserved parking
- Staff parking
- Motorcycle parking
- Bicycle parking
- EV charging spaces
- Loading/service spaces

## 5. Traffic Circulation

Vehicle Entry → Access Zone → Drive Aisle → Parking Space → Circulation → Exit

Manage:

- One-way circulation
- Two-way circulation
- Turning radius
- Drive aisle width
- Intersections
- Conflict points
- Ramps
- Queuing
- Congestion

## 6. Parking Occupancy

Monitor each Parking Access Zone based on:

- Total spaces
- Occupied spaces
- Available spaces
- Occupancy percentage
- Peak-hour demand

Example:

PAZ-E: 90% occupied → redirect incoming vehicles to PAZ-S.

## 7. Parking Guidance

Use:

- Parking availability signs
- Directional signs
- Zone identification
- Level identification
- Space sensors
- Digital displays
- Wayfinding
- License-plate recognition

## 8. Pedestrian Accessibility

After parking, provide a clear connection:

Parking Space → Pedestrian Route → Building Entrance

Consider:

- Sidewalks
- Crosswalks
- Accessible routes
- PWD ramps
- Elevators
- Stairs
- Escalators
- Lighting
- Separation from vehicle traffic

## 9. Parking Management Hierarchy

COMMERCIAL BUILDING / MALL
↓
PARKING FACILITY
↓
PARKING ACCESS ZONE
↓
PARKING LEVEL / AREA
↓
PARKING ROW
↓
PARKING SPACE

Example:

Mall Parking Facility → PAZ-E → Level 2 → Row E → Space E-24

## 10. Overall Management Categories

| Category | Main Function |
| --- | --- |
| Access Management | Control vehicle entry and exit |
| Zone Management | Organize North/East/South/West areas |
| Traffic Management | Control vehicle circulation |
| Space Management | Assign and classify parking spaces |
| Occupancy Management | Monitor available/occupied spaces |
| Guidance Management | Direct drivers to available spaces |
| Pedestrian Management | Provide safe routes to the building |
| Security Management | CCTV, lighting, patrols, emergency response |
| Payment Management | Tickets, cashless payment, validation |
| Operations & Maintenance | Markings, lighting, cleaning, equipment |

## Core concept

Parking Access Zone (PAZ) is the main organizing unit.

Each zone is evaluated by:

Entrance Accessibility + Circulation Efficiency + Parking Availability + Exit Accessibility + Pedestrian Connectivity

This gives you a structured way to evaluate North, East, South, and West parking areas of a mall based on vehicle accessibility and parking management.
