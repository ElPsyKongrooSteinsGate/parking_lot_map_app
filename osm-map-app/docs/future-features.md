# Future Features Roadmap

This document lists features that can be added in future versions of the parking management application.

## 1. Authentication and Authorization

- Production authentication and secure session management
- User and role management
- RBAC, ABAC, and PBAC enforcement
- Facility and parking-zone assignments
- Permit management
- Account activation and suspension
- Authorization audit logs

## 2. Parking Management Hierarchy

- Commercial facilities
- Parking facilities
- Parking Access Zones (PAZ-N, PAZ-E, PAZ-S, and PAZ-W)
- Parking levels
- Parking areas
- Parking rows
- Individual parking spaces
- Hierarchical space naming

## 3. Parking Space Management

- Regular parking spaces
- PWD/accessibility spaces
- Senior citizen spaces
- Family spaces
- VIP and reserved spaces
- Staff spaces
- Motorcycle spaces
- Bicycle spaces
- EV charging spaces
- Loading and service spaces
- Space reservations
- Space permits
- Space availability controls

## 4. Vehicle Access Management

- Vehicle entrances and exits
- Entry and exit lanes
- Drive aisles
- Turning areas
- Queuing areas
- Emergency access routes
- Pedestrian crossings
- Lane and access restrictions

## 5. Traffic and Circulation Management

- One-way and two-way circulation
- Turning-radius data
- Drive-aisle width management
- Ramps and vertical circulation
- Intersections and conflict points
- Bottleneck detection
- Congestion monitoring
- Peak-hour demand analysis
- Automatic vehicle redirection
- Traffic-flow visualization

## 6. Occupancy Management

- Real-time parking sensors
- Zone-level occupancy
- Level-level occupancy
- Occupancy percentages
- Peak-hour occupancy reports
- Availability predictions
- Low-availability alerts
- Automatic occupancy-based routing

## 7. Parking Guidance and Wayfinding

- Parking availability signs
- Directional signs
- Zone identification
- Level identification
- Space sensors
- Digital displays
- Dynamic wayfinding
- Mobile navigation
- Route recommendations
- Driver notifications

## 8. Pedestrian Accessibility

- Sidewalk mapping
- Crosswalk mapping
- Accessible pedestrian routes
- PWD ramps
- Elevators
- Stairs
- Escalators
- Building entrance connectivity
- Lighting and safety information
- Separation from vehicle traffic

## 9. Security Management

- CCTV integration
- License-plate recognition
- Security patrol tracking
- Emergency response workflows
- Incident reporting
- Suspicious-activity alerts
- Access logs
- Authorization audit trails
- Emergency notifications

## 10. Payment Management

- Parking tickets
- Cashless payments
- Online payments
- Digital receipts
- Validation codes
- Discount management
- Monthly parking passes
- Refund processing
- Payment history
- Revenue reporting

## 11. Operations and Maintenance

- Lighting maintenance schedules
- Cleaning schedules
- Line-marking management
- Sensor maintenance
- Equipment status monitoring
- Maintenance work orders
- Staff assignments
- Maintenance notifications
- Fault reporting
- Preventive maintenance planning

## 12. Reports and Administration

- Occupancy reports
- Revenue reports
- Traffic reports
- Security reports
- Maintenance reports
- User activity reports
- CSV and PDF export
- Analytics dashboards
- Scheduled reports
- Notifications and alerts

## 13. External Integrations

- Mapping and routing services
- IoT parking sensors
- Payment gateways
- CCTV systems
- License-plate recognition systems
- Smart parking signs
- Email and SMS providers
- Native mobile applications
- Property-management systems
- Municipal transportation systems

## Suggested Implementation Order

1. Complete the parking hierarchy and zone model.
2. Add parking-space types, permits, and reservations.
3. Integrate occupancy sensors and real-time availability.
4. Add payment and notification services.
5. Add traffic, pedestrian, and wayfinding features.
6. Add security, maintenance, reporting, and external integrations.
